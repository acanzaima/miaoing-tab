MiaowingTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to MiaowingTab. MiaowingTab is a browser extension that replaces the new tab page and helps users customize website shortcuts, wallpapers, search, widgets, and local data backups. We value your privacy and strive to keep data processing local, transparent, and limited to what is necessary.

You remain in control of your data. Persistent data—including new tab settings, website shortcuts, notes, to-do items, search history, and uploaded files—stays in your browser or is saved to a local location you choose when you create a backup. When you use online features, MiaowingTab sends only the minimum information required to provide the current feature to the third-party public services or static resource URLs listed below, such as the current search query, a URL you entered, a city query, or geographic coordinates. Local data unrelated to the current feature is not sent.

1. How We Collect and Use Information

MiaowingTab may process information in the following situations:

  • New tab settings: This includes themes, wallpapers, sidebar categories, website shortcuts, folders, scenes and scheduled switching plans, widget settings, search engine preferences, link-opening behavior, and similar settings used to restore your personalized new tab page.
  • Local content: This includes wallpapers and icons you upload, notes, to-do items, countdowns, and backup files. This content is used only for display, caching, backup, or restore features that you choose to use.
  • System password and device verification: System password creation and verification are performed locally. The extension persistently stores a one-way password verifier with a random salt, not the plaintext password. When a system password is created, the extension uses the browser to download a local TXT recovery file containing the plaintext password. You control that file, and it is not retained as application storage. Only after you actively enable device verification does the extension invoke a WebAuthn platform authenticator and locally store the credential identifier, public key, algorithm, transports, password-binding digest, and creation time. The private key remains with the operating system, authenticator, or password manager. The extension does not read or store biometric data such as fingerprints or facial scans.
  • Recently visited records: When you open a website shortcut from the new tab page, the extension locally records the corresponding URL, title, icon, click count, and most recent visit time. This information is used only by the Recent Sites widget and is not uploaded.
  • Search-related information: When you perform a search, the current query is sent to the search engine you selected. If search history is enabled, search queries are stored locally. If search suggestions are enabled, the query being typed is also sent to that search engine's suggestion service to retrieve suggestions.
  • Weather information: When you enter a city in the Weather widget, the city query is sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve matching cities and geocoding results. After you select a location, its latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather information. If you actively choose to use your current location, the browser's location result may also be sent to a geocoding service to identify your region.
  • Trending topics: When you use the Trending Topics widget, the extension requests the applicable trending-list API to retrieve the latest list.
  • Daily poetry: When daily recommended poetry is enabled in clean mode, the extension requests public content services such as Jinrishici to retrieve the content shown that day. Poetry or short-quote lists that you maintain yourself are stored locally only.
  • Website and icon information: When you add a website, online icon, or online wallpaper, the extension may request the URL you entered to retrieve a website icon, determine the resource type, or load and cache an image or video you selected.
  • Browser bookmarks: After you actively select bookmark sync and grant permission, the extension reads your browser bookmarks and imports their URLs and titles into your new tab shortcuts.
  • Announcements and recommended resources: When the extension starts, it may check announcements published by the developer. When you open the Wallpaper Center, the extension may retrieve a list of recommended wallpapers and the related resources. These requests do not include your new tab settings, notes, to-do items, search history, bookmarks, or uploaded files.

2. How Information Is Stored

MiaowingTab primarily stores data using browser-provided local storage capabilities, including localStorage, IndexedDB, the Cache API, and browser extension local storage. By default, the persistent data described above remains on your device and in your browser environment. It is not uploaded to the developer's servers or a third-party cloud service as account or synchronization data, except for the minimum information required when you use online features.

If you uninstall the extension, clear browser data, or manually restore or overwrite a backup, the relevant local data may be deleted or replaced. Please make sure you have an appropriate backup before clearing data, restoring a backup, or uninstalling the extension.

Full backups and automatic undo records may contain the salted system-password verifier and protected-action settings so the corresponding local protection state can be restored. They do not contain the plaintext system password or the local WebAuthn device credential. Re-downloadable online icon caches are excluded from full backups, while local wallpapers and local icons that are still in use may be included in a backup you actively create. This local data remains in the current browser environment until you delete, clear, or overwrite it, or uninstall the extension.

3. Permissions We Use

  • storage / unlimitedStorage: Used to save new tab settings, user-uploaded resources, cached icons, backup and restore data, and widget content.
  • Host access: Used to request search suggestions, website icons, online wallpapers, online icons, weather data, trending topics, daily poetry, announcements, and recommended resources.
  • Browser location: Used only when you actively select “Use Current Location” in the Weather widget. Location permission is displayed and managed by your browser.
  • Bookmarks: Used only after you actively enable bookmark sync and grant permission, so the extension can read bookmarks and import their URLs and titles into the new tab page.
  • Website icons: Used to retrieve the corresponding website icon when you add a URL or sync bookmarks. If this capability is unavailable or permission has not been granted, the extension uses another icon source or a text-based icon.
  • WebAuthn platform verification: Invoked only when you actively enable or use device verification. The browser, operating system, or password manager displays the verification interface and manages the private key. When device verification is reset or disabled, the extension may ask the platform to mark the old credential as no longer in use.

4. Third-Party Services

When you use certain online features, your requests may be sent to third-party services:

  • Search suggestions: sent to your currently selected search engine.
  • Weather services: sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather and city geocoding data. Current-location identification may be sent to geocoding services such as BigDataCloud.
  • Trending topics: sent to trending-data APIs such as 60s API to retrieve content from the source you selected.
  • Daily poetry: sent to public content services such as Jinrishici to retrieve daily poetry recommendations.
  • Website icons, online wallpapers, and online icons: sent to the target website that you entered or selected.
  • Announcements and recommended resources: retrieved through content delivery services such as jsDelivr and the GitHub CDN for developer-maintained announcements, wallpaper lists, and related resources.

When you access these third-party services, they may process standard network information—such as your IP address, User-Agent, and request time—under their own privacy policies. Other than a search query, URL, city query, or geographic coordinates required for the current feature, MiaowingTab does not intentionally attach unrelated new tab settings, notes, to-do items, search history, bookmarks, or uploaded files to these requests. MiaowingTab does not control how third-party services process data.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not upload your complete new tab settings, notes, to-do items, search history list, or imported bookmark data to the cloud or another third-party service as account or synchronization data.
  • We do not inject advertising tracking code into web pages.
  • We do not read your bookmarks without browser permission.
  • We do not use data obtained through browser permissions for personalized advertising, resale, or purposes unrelated to features that you actively use.

MiaowingTab's use of data obtained through browser permission APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Such data is used only to provide or improve features that you actively use and is not made available for human review unless you give explicit consent for specific data, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.

6. Data Control and Deletion

In the extension settings, you can disable search history, clear search records, and modify or delete website shortcuts, widgets, and local resources. You can also delete locally stored data through your browser's extension management or site-data controls, or by uninstalling the extension.

You can also change or remove the system password and reset or disable device verification. Disabling device verification stops the extension from using the locally stored credential. Whether an old passkey remains visible in the browser, operating system, or password manager is controlled by that platform.

7. Children's Privacy

MiaowingTab is not intended to collect personal information from minors. If you are a minor, please use this extension under the guidance of a parent or legal guardian.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in response to changes in features, laws and regulations, or browser store policies. The updated policy will be published on this page, and the date shown at the top of the page will indicate the latest update.
